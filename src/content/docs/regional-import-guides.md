# Regional Import Guides

This page consolidates the market-by-market reference previously split across five thin
country documents (EU Import Guide, Germany Import, UK Import, Australia Import, UAE & Saudi
Import). One region, one table — so you can compare destinations at a glance. (The separate
[Import Overview](/docs/import-overview/) process walkthrough remains.)

The numbers are China EV Mart's tariff records (last verified 15 September 2026). They are a
**reference, not a quote**: confirm current rates with a customs broker before committing funds.

## How the tariff stack works

The landed cost of a Chinese EV is a **compounded** sequence, not a sum:

1. **Standard import duty** — a percentage of the CIF value (car + insurance + freight).
2. **Countervailing duty (CVD)** — an anti-subsidy tariff layered on the duty-paid value.
   EU markets only, BEV only, and producer-specific (BYD 17% · Geely group 18.8% · SAIC 35.3% ·
   Tesla Shanghai 7.8% · other cooperating producers 20.7%). Non-EU markets and PHEV/EREV pay none.
3. **VAT / GST** — charged last, on the duty-inclusive running total.
4. **Fixed block** — freight, clearance, certification, registration, inland transport.

The full formula and worked examples live in the [Landed Cost Methodology](/docs/landed-cost-methodology/)
and, for interactive per-market pricing, on the [electricvehiclehub.net](https://electricvehiclehub.net/landed-cost-calculator/) sister site.

Certification: **EU Whole Vehicle Type Approval (WVTA)** for series imports, or national
single-vehicle approval (e.g. Germany §21 StVZO) for individual imports. Budget $1,500–$5,000
and 2–6 weeks. See [EU Homologation](/docs/eu-homologation/).

### Non-EU Europe (3 markets)

| Market | Standard duty | CVD | VAT | Registration (model) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| United Kingdom | 10% | none | 20% | $400 |
| Norway | 0% | none | 25% | $1,400 |
| Switzerland | 4% | none | 8.1% | $200 |

Certification: UK uses **Individual Vehicle Approval (IVA)** via the DVSA; Switzerland and
Norway follow their own approval paths for non-EU vehicles.
