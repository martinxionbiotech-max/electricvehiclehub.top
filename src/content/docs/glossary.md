# Glossary of EV Import Terms

The language of vehicle importing is dense with acronyms and terms of art. This glossary defines the ones you'll actually encounter, in plain English.

## Cost & pricing terms

**FOB (Free on Board)** — The price of the vehicle at the origin port, excluding ocean freight and insurance. The seller's responsibility ends when the vehicle is loaded on the ship.

**CIF (Cost, Insurance, and Freight)** — The price including ocean freight and insurance to the destination port. This is the value that customs duties are calculated on.

**Base price** — The factory or ex-works price of the vehicle in China, before any freight, duty, or tax.

**Landed cost** — The total cost to import a vehicle to a specific country and make it road-legal, before dealer margin. Base price + duty + countervailing duty + VAT/GST + freight + clearance + certification + registration + inland transport.

**Premium over base** — The percentage increase from base price to landed cost. A vehicle at $24,690 landing at $44,413 has a +79.9% premium.

## Trade & tariff terms

**Standard import duty** — The basic customs duty on a vehicle, a percentage of the CIF value. 10% in the EU and UK for Chinese EVs; 5% in the Gulf; 0% for EVs in Australia.

**Most-favoured-nation (MFN) duty** — The standard duty rate a country applies to imports from countries with which it has normal (non-preferential) trade relations.

**Countervailing duty (CVD)** — An additional anti-subsidy tariff applied on top of the standard duty, designed to offset subsidies a foreign government gives its exporters. For Chinese EVs in the EU, it is producer-specific: 7.8% to 35.3%.

**Anti-dumping duty** — A separate tariff designed to counter goods sold below fair market value. Distinct from, and additional to, a countervailing duty.

**Code / TARIC** — The classification code under which a vehicle is declared to customs. Passenger EVs generally fall under Harmonized System heading 8703.

## Tax terms

**VAT (Value-Added Tax)** — The consumption tax applied in the EU and UK, charged last on the duty-inclusive total. Recoverable for VAT-registered businesses.

**GST (Goods and Services Tax)** — The equivalent consumption tax in Australia (and other jurisdictions). 10% in Australia.

**VAT/GST recoverability** — The ability of a registered business to reclaim VAT/GST, making it a cash-flow item rather than a final cost.

## Freight & logistics terms

**RoRo (Roll-on/Roll-off)** — A shipping method where vehicles are driven onto and off a vessel. The standard method for shipping cars; lower cost and lower risk of damage than containerised.

**Containerised shipping** — Shipping a vehicle inside a standard shipping container. More expensive, but sometimes required for high-value or unusual vehicles.

**Customs clearance** — The administrative process of filing an entry and settling duties to release a vehicle from customs.

## Compliance & certification terms

**Type approval** — Certification that a vehicle type meets all applicable technical requirements of a market.

**WVTA (Whole Vehicle Type Approval)** — The EU's full type-approval route for series imports.

**Small Series Type Approval** — A relaxed EU approval route for limited annual volumes.

**Homologation** — The process of certifying a vehicle as compliant with a market's technical regulations.

**§21 StVZO** — The German single-vehicle approval route, via a technical inspection organisation (TÜV, DEKRA, GTÜ).

**IVA (Individual Vehicle Approval)** — The UK's single-vehicle approval scheme, administered by the DVSA.

**ADR (Australian Design Rules)** — The technical standards imported vehicles must meet in Australia.

**RAV (Register of Approved Vehicles)** — Australia's register of vehicles approved for road use.

**COC (Certificate of Conformity)** — A document certifying that a vehicle conforms to an approved type.

## Range & specification terms

**CLTC (China Light-duty vehicle Test Cycle)** — China's range test standard, which typically reports 15–30% higher figures than real-world use.

**WLTP (Worldwide Harmonised Light vehicles Test Procedure)** — The EU's range test standard, closer to real-world conditions.

**NEDC (New European Driving Cycle)** — An older EU test standard, now largely superseded by WLTP.
