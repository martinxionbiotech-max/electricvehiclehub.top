## Overview

The BYD Seal 06 GT is a rear-wheel-drive battery-electric sedan from BYD. As a mainstream-value sedan battery-electric, it starts at $19,270 ex-factory. It carries 505 km of CLTC range, a 59 kWh battery, and MacPherson strut front / Multi-link rear suspension. On landed cost for this sedan battery-electric, Germany applies a 17% countervailing duty on top of the 10% standard tariff — the least-costly destination is Qatar, near +26.7% over base ($24,413 landed). As a sedan battery-electric, it is aimed at private buyers and ride-hailing operators focused on volume import economics.
