Changan is the core consumer-car brand of Chongqing Changan Automobile, a mass-market name focused on making electrified powertrains and connectivity accessible. We catalogue 14 ChangAn models, whose factory prices span roughly $8,400 to $26,460; in Germany, landed cost runs about $17,596 to $41,236, a +56% to +113% premium over the sticker price, with its BEV models carrying a 20.7% EU countervailing duty.
