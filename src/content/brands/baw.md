BAW (Beijing Automobile Works) is a historic Chinese marque with off-road and utilitarian DNA, now backed by Shandong Weiqiao Pioneering Group. The catalogue lists 4 BAW models at factory prices of roughly $4,130 to $23,580; in Germany those land near $13,125 to $42,165, for a +59% to +218% premium over factory pricing — a 20.7% EU countervailing duty applies to its BEV models.
