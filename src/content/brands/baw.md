BAW (Beijing Automobile Works) is a historic Chinese automotive brand with roots dating back to 1951. Renowned for its rugged engineering and utilitarian DNA, BAW has long specialized in off-road, military, and practical vehicles. Backed by Shandong Weiqiao Pioneering Group, the brand is entering a new era focused on modernization, electrification, and reliable mobility solutions for diverse global markets.
