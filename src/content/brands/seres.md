Seres is the electric vehicle brand of Seres Group (formerly Sokon), a Chongqing-based manufacturer founded in 1986. Launched in 2016 and refocused as the group's export marque from 2023, Seres produces premium electric SUVs built on Seres Group's electrification platform, positioned for international markets while the AITO brand serves the domestic premium segment.
