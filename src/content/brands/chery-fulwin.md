Chery Fulwin is Chery Automobile's new-energy sub-brand, revived in 2023 from the Fulwin name once tied to Chery's early compact-car success. Our Chery Fulwin line-up spans 14 models, priced from around $11,250 to $26,750 ex-factory; Germany landed cost is about $21,326 to $41,616, a +56% to +111% premium after a 20.7% EU countervailing duty on the BEV portion of the line-up.
