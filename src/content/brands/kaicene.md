Kaicene is a commercial vehicle brand under Changan Automobile, dedicated to providing professional, reliable, and intelligent mobility solutions for business users worldwide. Established in 2019, Kaicene (also known internationally as Changan Kaicene) builds upon Changan’s strong engineering foundation to serve the light truck, pickup, and van segments with products that combine durability, efficiency, and modern technology.
