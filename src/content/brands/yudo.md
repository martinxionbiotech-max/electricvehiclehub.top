Yudo is a pure-electric vehicle brand founded in 2015 and headquartered in Putian, Fujian. Backed by local and automotive-industry investors, Yudo focuses on compact electric SUVs and crossovers for urban and family use, positioning itself as an affordable entry point into the Chinese electric vehicle market.
