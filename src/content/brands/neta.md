Neta, also known as Nezha, is Hozon Auto's consumer EV brand, founded in 2014 around value-focused sedans and SUVs. The 5 Neta models we track carry factory prices from roughly $8,320 to $21,500; landed in Germany, that becomes about $19,745 to $40,569, a +68% to +137% uplift on a 20.7% EU countervailing duty for its BEV models.
