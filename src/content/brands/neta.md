Neta (Nezha) is the consumer EV brand of Hozon Auto, a Chinese electric vehicle maker founded in 2014 and headquartered in Jiaxing, Zhejiang. Neta targets value-focused buyers with compact and mid-size electric sedans and SUVs, pairing aggressive pricing with smart-cabin features to compete against larger domestic rivals and in export markets.
