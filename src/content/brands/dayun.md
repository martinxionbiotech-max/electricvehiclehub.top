# Dayun Yuanhang

Dayun Yuanhang is the electric passenger vehicle brand of Dayun Group, a commercial vehicle and motorcycle conglomerate founded in 1987 and headquartered in Yuncheng, Shanxi. Launched in 2022, Yuanhang (meaning "long voyage") debuted a line of large electric sedans and SUVs aimed at the premium domestic market.
