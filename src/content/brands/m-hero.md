# M-Hero

M-Hero is a high-performance off-road new-energy vehicle brand under Dongfeng Motor Corporation, created to redefine strength, intelligence, and adventure in the electrified era. Officially launched in 2022, M-Hero — short for “Mengshi Hero” — inherits the rugged DNA of Dongfeng’s legendary military vehicles while embracing the future of electric power and intelligent technology.
