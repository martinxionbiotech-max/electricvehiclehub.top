SAIC is a premium intelligent electric vehicle brand jointly developed by SAIC Motor Corporation Limited and Huawei, operating under the Harmony Intelligent Mobility Alliance (HIMA) framework. Officially unveiled in April 2025, SAIC — also known by its Chinese name Shangjie — represents the fusion of SAIC’s world-class automotive manufacturing expertise and Huawei’s cutting-edge intelligent vehicle technology.
