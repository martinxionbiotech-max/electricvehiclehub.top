SAIC, known in Chinese as Shangjie, is the premium intelligent EV brand from SAIC Motor and Huawei under the HIMA framework, unveiled in 2025. Across the 2 SAIC models we catalogue, factory prices run from roughly $22,190 to $22,510; landed in Germany they reach about $35,647 to $46,467, a +61% to +106% import premium, with its BEV models carrying a 35.3% EU countervailing duty.
