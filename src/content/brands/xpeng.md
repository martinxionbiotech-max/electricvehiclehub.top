XPeng is a Guangzhou-headquartered smart-EV brand founded in 2014, best known for its in-house XNGP advanced driver-assistance stack and its push into autonomous-driving features on mainstream-priced cars. The brand sits in the upper-mid of China's electric ladder: our catalogue tracks ten XPeng models, with factory prices spanning roughly $16,800 to $43,630 and landed costs in Germany running from about $33,143 to $75,534 — a premium of +48% to +97% over the Chinese sticker price, on XPeng's 20.7% EU countervailing-duty rate.
