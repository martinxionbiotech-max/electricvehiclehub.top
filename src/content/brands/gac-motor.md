GAC Motor, the international face of Trumpchi, is GAC Group's flagship passenger brand, developing premium vehicles since 2010. Our catalogue tracks 11 GAC Motor models, with factory prices spanning roughly $18,030 to $45,460 and landed costs in Germany running from about $30,201 to $66,107 — a premium of +45% to +68% over the Chinese sticker price, since these PHEV/EREV/HEV powertrains fall outside the EU countervailing-duty scope.
