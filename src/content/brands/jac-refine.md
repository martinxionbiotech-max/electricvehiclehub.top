Refine is a sub-brand of JAC Motors (Anhui Jianghuai Automobile Group Corp., Ltd.), specializing in multi-purpose vehicles (MPVs), passenger vans, and light commercial vehicles. Since its debut in 2002, Refine has become one of China’s most recognizable names in the MPV segment, known for its combination of practicality, reliability, and comfort.
