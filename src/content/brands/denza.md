Denza is the premium new-energy brand jointly developed by BYD and Mercedes-Benz, combining Chinese electrification with German luxury standards. The 9 Denza models we track carry factory prices from roughly $36,590 to $54,900; landed in Germany, that becomes about $61,879 to $90,681, a +45% to +71% uplift on a 17.0% EU countervailing duty for its BEV models.
