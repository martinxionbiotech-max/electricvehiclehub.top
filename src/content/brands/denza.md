DENZA is a premium new-energy vehicle brand jointly developed by BYD and Mercedes-Benz, combining Chinese innovation with German luxury craftsmanship. Established in 2010 and revitalized in 2022 under BYD’s leadership, DENZA represents a fusion of intelligent technology, refined design, and sustainable performance aimed at redefining the high-end electric mobility experience.
