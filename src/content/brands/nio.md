NIO is a premium Chinese electric vehicle brand founded in 2014 and headquartered in Hefei, best known for its battery-swapping network (NIO Power) and its user-community model, which together define its ownership experience. It sits at the premium end of the catalogue: our data tracks ten NIO models, with factory prices spanning roughly $41,850 to $108,170 and landed costs in Germany running from about $72,721 to $177,505 — a premium of +64% to +74% over the Chinese sticker price, on NIO's 20.7% EU countervailing-duty rate.
