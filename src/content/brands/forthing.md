Forthing, also known as Dongfeng Fengxing, is the passenger-vehicle brand of Dongfeng Liuzhou Motor, focused on practical family vehicles since 2001. The catalogue lists 11 Forthing models at factory prices of roughly $12,490 to $25,340; in Germany those land near $26,334 to $40,395, for a +57% to +111% premium over factory pricing on a 20.7% EU countervailing duty for its BEV models.
