Xiaomi Auto is the intelligent EV brand of Xiaomi Corporation, launched in 2021 to extend the company's ecosystem from smart devices to smart mobility. We catalogue 6 Xiaomi models, whose factory prices span roughly $29,150 to $73,600; in Germany, landed cost runs about $44,757 to $122,885, a +48% to +80% premium over the sticker price after a 20.7% EU countervailing duty on the BEV portion of the line-up.
