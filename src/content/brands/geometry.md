Geometry (Jihe) is Geely's dedicated electric vehicle brand, launched in 2019 to lead the group's pure-battery portfolio. Based in Hangzhou, Geometry offers a range of value-oriented electric sedans and SUVs designed for urban commuting and fleet use, positioned as an accessible entry into Geely's electrified lineup.
