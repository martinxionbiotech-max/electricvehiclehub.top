ChangAn NEVO is Changan Automobile's global new-energy brand, launched in 2023 to lead the group's international electrification push. ChangAn NEVO contributes 10 models to the catalogue, factory-priced from about $5,300 to $30,970 and landing in Germany around $14,974 to $55,531 — a +52% to +182% import premium, with its BEV models carrying a 20.7% EU countervailing duty.
