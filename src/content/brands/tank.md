# Tank

Tank is an off-road and adventure-focused SUV brand under Great Wall Motor (GWM), created to combine rugged capability with modern intelligence and luxury. Officially launched in 2021 as an independent brand, Tank evolved from GWM’s off-road heritage to become China’s leading name in premium all-terrain vehicles designed for both exploration and everyday comfort.
