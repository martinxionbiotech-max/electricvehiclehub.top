# Arcfox

Arcfox is BAIC Group's electric vehicle brand, launched in 2017 under BAIC BluePark New Energy Technology and based in Beijing. It sits at the premium end of BAIC's electric line-up, and several models use intelligent cockpit and driver-assistance technology developed with Huawei. Our catalogue tracks seven Arcfox models, with factory prices spanning roughly $8,850 to $36,170 and landed costs in Germany running from about $20,363 to $62,848 — a premium of +74% to +130% over the Chinese sticker price.
