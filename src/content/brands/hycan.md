Hycan was an electric-vehicle brand from the GAC and NIO joint venture, announced in 2019 and wound down in 2025, still catalogued here for reference. We catalogue 4 Hycan models, whose factory prices span roughly $18,440 to $44,280; in Germany, landed cost runs about $35,735 to $76,561, a +73% to +94% premium over the sticker price, reflecting a 20.7% EU countervailing-duty rate.
