# Hycan

Hycan (Hechuang) was an electric vehicle brand created by a joint venture between GAC Group and NIO, announced in 2019 and headquartered in Guangzhou. The brand fielded a small lineup of smart electric sedans, SUVs, and MPVs before ceasing operations in 2025, and remains cataloged here for historical and import reference.
