Haval is Great Wall Motor's global SUV brand, established in 2013 and among China's most recognised sport-utility names. Haval fields 7 models in our catalogue, priced from roughly $15,530 to $24,480 at the factory gate and landing in Germany between about $26,929 to $38,644, for a +58% to +73% premium, since these PHEV/EREV/HEV powertrains fall outside the EU countervailing-duty scope.
