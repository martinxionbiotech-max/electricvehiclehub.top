Fangchengbao, literally “Formula Leopard”, is a premium off-road and performance new-energy vehicle brand under BYD Group, launched in 2023. Positioned between BYD’s mainstream models and its luxury sub-brand Yangwang, Fangchengbao combines rugged capability with advanced electrified technology to create vehicles that are both adventurous and intelligent.
