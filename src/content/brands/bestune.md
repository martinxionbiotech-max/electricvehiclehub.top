Bestune, formerly Besturn, is FAW Group's mid-to-premium passenger brand, created around intelligent, high-value family vehicles. The 7 Bestune models we track carry factory prices from roughly $3,500 to $27,330; landed in Germany, that becomes about $12,130 to $49,780, a +78% to +247% uplift — a 20.7% EU countervailing duty applies to its BEV models.
