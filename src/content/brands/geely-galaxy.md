Geely Galaxy is Geely Auto's premium new-energy brand, launched in 2023 around the group's latest hybrid and pure-electric technology. We catalogue 19 Geely Galaxy models, whose factory prices span roughly $5,540 to $41,650; in Germany, landed cost runs about $15,215 to $71,370, a +48% to +175% premium over the sticker price after an 18.8% EU countervailing duty on the BEV portion of the line-up.
