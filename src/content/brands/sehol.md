Sehol (Sihao) is a new-energy vehicle brand of JAC Group, launched in 2018 and headquartered in Hefei, Anhui. Created in the wake of JAC's joint venture with Volkswagen, Sehol offers affordable electric hatchbacks, sedans, and SUVs that blend JAC's manufacturing scale with German-derived quality standards for the domestic and export markets.
