Sehol, known domestically as Sihao, is JAC Group's new-energy brand, launched in 2018 with German-derived quality standards. Our catalogue tracks 4 Sehol models, with factory prices spanning roughly $5,540 to $19,430 and landed costs in Germany running from about $15,353 to $37,299 — a premium of +92% to +177% over the Chinese sticker price after a 20.7% EU countervailing duty.
