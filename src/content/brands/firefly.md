Firefly is a new-energy vehicle (NEV) brand under SAIC Motor Corporation Limited, created to make electric mobility more accessible, youthful, and globally appealing. Officially launched in 2023, Firefly serves as SAIC’s next-generation brand for compact electric vehicles, focusing on intelligent design, efficiency, and affordability for urban drivers around the world.
