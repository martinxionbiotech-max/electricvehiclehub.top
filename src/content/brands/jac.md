JAC (Jianghuai Automobile) is the core brand of Anhui Jianghuai Automobile Group, grown since 1964 from commercial vehicles into a full car line. Across the 8 JAC models we catalogue, factory prices run from roughly $13,860 to $26,730; landed in Germany they reach about $24,743 to $45,988, a +56% to +99% import premium after a 20.7% EU countervailing duty on the BEV portion of the line-up.
