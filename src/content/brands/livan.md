# Livan

Livan (Ruilan) is a Geely-backed new-energy brand established in 2022 and headquartered in Chongqing. Built on the Lifan manufacturing base, Livan focuses on battery-swapping electric mobility alongside conventional electrified SUVs and MPVs, positioning itself for ride-hailing, fleet, and budget-conscious private buyers.
