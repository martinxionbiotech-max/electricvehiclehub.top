Wuling is one of China's most recognisable automotive brands, jointly operated by SAIC Motor, GM and Liuzhou Wuling Motors under the SGMW joint venture. Wuling fields 19 models in our catalogue, priced from roughly $5,000 to $18,280 at the factory gate and landing in Germany between about $15,455 to $32,493, for a +67% to +209% premium — a 35.3% EU countervailing duty applies to its BEV models.
