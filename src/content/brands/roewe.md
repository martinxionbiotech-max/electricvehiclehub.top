Roewe is SAIC Motor's premium passenger brand, established in 2006 as the company's first self-developed marque. Our catalogue tracks 12 Roewe models, with factory prices spanning roughly $13,770 to $36,590 and landed costs in Germany running from about $24,625 to $71,404 — a premium of +55% to +118% over the Chinese sticker price — a 35.3% EU countervailing duty applies to its BEV models.
