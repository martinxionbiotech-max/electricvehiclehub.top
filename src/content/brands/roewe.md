Roewe is a premium automotive brand under SAIC Motor Corp., Ltd., created to represent the company’s pursuit of innovation, intelligence, and refined mobility. Established in 2006, Roewe was introduced as SAIC’s first self-developed passenger car brand, combining global design excellence with advanced Chinese engineering to deliver vehicles that are both elegant and technologically forward.
