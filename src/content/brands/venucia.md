Venucia (Qichen) is the Chinese-brand marque of the Dongfeng-Nissan joint venture, launched in 2010 and headquartered in Guangzhou. It offers sedans and SUVs engineered on Nissan-derived platforms, with battery-electric and plug-in hybrid variants that combine joint-venture quality with local pricing for the Chinese and regional export markets.
